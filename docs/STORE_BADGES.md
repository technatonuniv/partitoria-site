# Store badges

The Google Play badges in `public/store-badges/` are unmodified PNGs from
Google's badge service, fetched on 2026-11-07:

`https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/{code}_badge_web_generic.png`

The filename language codes match the site's locales except Ukrainian (`uk`),
whose Google asset uses `ua`. The badge retains its original proportions and
links to the Partitoria Google Play listing. Google Play badge guidance:
https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google-play/google-play/lockups-icons-badges/

The footer badge group is vertical so another store badge can be added below
when a real listing exists. Leave room around each badge and follow the
respective store's current branding rules.
